# MotorGeeks brand-guide hero images

These ten hero images were generated for the MotorGeeks brand-guide pages with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool on 1 October 2026. They are editorial representations of motorcycle categories rather than photographs of specific manufacturer models. Manufacturer marks are deliberately kept separate from the imagery.

Shared prompt direction:

- Photorealistic editorial motorcycle photography for a wide website hero.
- A motorcycle category and colour treatment appropriate to the guide's subject.
- Motorcycle positioned on the right with quiet negative space on the left for page copy.
- Natural British or European road environments.
- No people, text, watermarks, number plates or manufacturer logos.
- Believable mechanical geometry, materials and road use.
